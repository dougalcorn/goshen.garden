Goshen Garden is a micro farm on a one-acre property in Goshen, Ohio. My goal is to grow enough food to sustain myself year-round on a whole-food, plant-based diet — and to build something that, over time, becomes a real business.

I'm starting small. No-till beds, honest soil work, and a lot of learning. I've kept a backyard kitchen garden, but I'm new to farming as an intentional discipline and business. I've been reading and thinking about market gardening for years and now I have moved to this property to start it.

The goal is a productive micro farm that generates real income, grows most of what I eat, and sees me through retirement.

The real motivation here is to partner with God in growing food. From the beginning, humanity was commissioned to work and keep the earth — to be participants in what God makes, not just consumers of it. Growing food is one of the most direct ways I know to answer that call.

Sharing food is intimate. When someone shares in my harvest, they are trusting me with something that matters: what goes on their table, what feeds their family. I do not take that lightly. I want to connect with who I grow for and by extension connect them to the land that produces their food.
