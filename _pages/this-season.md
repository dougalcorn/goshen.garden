## This Season — 2026

This is the first season at Goshen Garden, and it starts from scratch.

The land is cleared — about fifteen trees came down to open up the south garden site — but the ground isn't ready yet. Before any beds go in, a French drain needs to be installed to address drainage problems that would otherwise drown the garden in a wet season. That work comes first.

Once drainage is sorted, the plan is to install a deer and wildlife fence around the full garden perimeter — 63' wide, 42' deep — and begin building beds.

## The bed layout

![South garden layout overhead view](/assets/images/south-garden-layout.png)
*South garden — 63'×42' fenced area with ten 50' beds*

The south garden will have ten 50' beds, each 30" wide, with 24" pathways between them and a 4' perimeter path around the outside. The two outermost beds, running north/south along each side, will be permanent strawberry beds. The remaining eight beds run east/west.

This season I'll only cultivate half the interior beds. The other half will be tarped for most of the season to kill existing vegetation and begin building soil without tillage. In late fall those tarped areas will go into cover crops over winter. Next season the plan flips — the previously tarped beds get cultivated and the cultivated beds get tarped — completing the full garden buildout over two seasons.

## What I'm not doing yet

I'm not focusing yet on seed starting. The big job is getting the garden itself put in. I'll likely go buy transplants from a local nursery and direct seed initially. 

## What success looks like this year

Year one is about learning this land — what it can produce, which crops thrive here, how the beds perform. Before scaling, those questions need real answers.

The modest goal is to keep a small number of families supplied with fresh produce through the season. Not a business yet, but a proof of concept — real relationships, real food, real feedback. If I can do that reliably while getting the infrastructure right, that's a win worth building on.
